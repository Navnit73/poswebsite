You can't improve what you don't measure, but you also can't measure everything. A small pharmacy owner doesn't need a 40-metric dashboard. They need a handful of numbers that show whether cash tied up in stock is working or sitting idle.

These seven KPIs give you that picture. Each one has a formula, a worked example, and what to do when it moves the wrong way.

---

## 1. Inventory Turnover

**What it tells you:** how many times you sell through your average stock in a year.

```
Inventory turnover = Cost of goods sold (annual) ÷ Average inventory at cost
```

**Example:** COGS of $1,200,000 and average inventory of $110,000 → **10.9 turns a year**.

For independent community pharmacies, benchmarks of around **10–12 turns a year** are commonly cited, though front-store retail lines usually turn more slowly than prescription stock. Track your own trend rather than chasing a single benchmark.

**If it's low:** you're holding too much stock. Review par levels and slow movers.

---

## 2. Days of Inventory on Hand

**What it tells you:** how many days your current stock would last at normal sales.

```
Days on hand = 365 ÷ Inventory turnover
```

**Example:** 365 ÷ 10.9 = **33 days**.

This is the same information as turnover, expressed in a way that's easier to explain to staff. If your wholesaler delivers daily, holding 60+ days of a fast-moving line rarely makes sense.

---

## 3. GMROI (Gross Margin Return on Inventory Investment)

**What it tells you:** how much gross profit each dollar of stock generates.

```
GMROI = Annual gross margin ($) ÷ Average inventory at cost
```

**Example:** Gross margin of $300,000 on average inventory of $110,000 → **GMROI 2.7**. Every $1 in stock generates $2.70 of gross profit a year.

GMROI is particularly useful for **front-store categories**. A vitamin range with a high margin percentage but slow sales may have a lower GMROI than a fast-moving, lower-margin first-aid range.

---

## 4. Expiry Write-Off Rate

**What it tells you:** how much stock you lose to expiry.

```
Expiry write-off rate = Value of expired stock written off ÷ Average inventory at cost
```

**Example:** $2,800 written off ÷ $110,000 = **2.5%**.

A well-run small pharmacy with FEFO and expiry alerts should be able to get this **below 1%**. If it's higher, start with our **[expired medicine returns guide](/blog/pharmacy-expired-medicine-returns-guide)** and **[FEFO vs FIFO explainer](/blog/fefo-vs-fifo-pharmacy-inventory)**.

---

## 5. Dead Stock Percentage

**What it tells you:** how much of your stock hasn't sold in a long time.

```
Dead stock % = Value of products with zero sales in 180 days ÷ Total inventory value
```

**Example:** $9,000 of zero-movement stock in a $110,000 inventory → **8.2%**.

Dead stock is cash you can't use, and it's the stock most likely to expire. Review the list monthly: return what you can, discount retail lines, and stop reordering.

---

## 6. Stock-Out Rate

**What it tells you:** how often a customer asks for something you don't have.

```
Stock-out rate = Number of requests you couldn't fill ÷ Total requests
```

This is harder to measure because missed sales don't show up in your POS. A simple method: keep an "owings / couldn't supply" log at the counter for four weeks.

Every stock-out is a customer who might try a competitor, and for prescription patients that may become a permanent switch. If the rate is high on specific lines, raise their **[reorder points](/blog/pharmacy-reorder-point-par-levels)**.

---

## 7. Shrinkage Rate

**What it tells you:** stock lost to theft, damage, or recording errors.

```
Shrinkage rate = (Recorded stock − Counted stock) at cost ÷ Total inventory at cost
```

Measure it at every **[stocktake](/blog/pharmacy-stocktake-guide)** and cycle count. Persistent shrinkage on small, high-value items such as nicotine replacement, fragrances, and some OTC lines usually points to theft. Random variances across many lines usually point to process errors.

## Your Monthly KPI Scorecard

| KPI | Formula | Healthy Direction | Review |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Inventory turnover | COGS ÷ Avg inventory | ↑ Higher | Quarterly |
| Days on hand | 365 ÷ Turnover | ↓ Lower | Quarterly |
| GMROI | Gross margin ÷ Avg inventory | ↑ Higher | Quarterly, by category |
| Expiry write-off | Expired value ÷ Avg inventory | ↓ Below 1% | Monthly |
| Dead stock | Zero-movement value ÷ Total | ↓ Lower | Monthly |
| Stock-out rate | Unfilled ÷ Total requests | ↓ Lower | Monthly |
| Shrinkage | Variance ÷ Total inventory | ↓ Lower | Each count |
